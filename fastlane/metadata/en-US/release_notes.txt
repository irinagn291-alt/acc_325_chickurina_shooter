Version 1.0 is the initial release of Chicken Shoot. It includes timed target shooting with a two-minute clock and five shots, wind speed and direction, the Boost Machine with No Wind, Maximum Approach, and + 20%, How to Play, SETTINGS for MUSIC, SOUND, and HAPTIC, 45 achievements, and guns and sights. The first gun and the first sight start unlocked. Music, sound, and haptic feedback start on. Scores, settings, unlocks, and achievement progress stay on this device.
