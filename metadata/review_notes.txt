Chicken Shoot is a portrait target-shooting game. You aim a gun at a target, allow for wind, and try to score as many points as you can before the clock or your shots run out. It is for people who want short rounds of timed target practice, with unlockable guns and sights and a list of achievements. No credentials or demo account are needed.

A cold start shows a logo video, with no title and no buttons, for about three seconds, then the main menu. The menu shows COLLECTED 0/45, TUTORIAL, and SHOOT. Music, sound, and haptic feedback start on. SHOOT on a first launch does not start a round. It opens How to Play. The first card shows SHOOTER CLUB, HOW TO PLAY, and TELL ME MORE!. The last card uses DONE. DONE closes How to Play and remembers that it was finished. TUTORIAL on the menu opens the same How to Play at any time. After DONE, SHOOT hides the menu and does not open a round or the LOADING... view. There is no second button on the menu that starts the match. A loading view shows LOADING... and a filling bar, then opens the shooting round. Before shooting, a Boost Machine runs by itself: the reels spin, stop after a few seconds, and the round starts. You do not have to tap to spin. The status line starts as Let's Start!. A clock counts down from two minutes as mm:ss and turns red in the last ten seconds. Five shot markers are shown and disappear as you fire. Aim by touching and holding, moving the sight, then releasing. A faster aim scores more. Three boosts can be granted for the next shot: No Wind, Maximum Approach, or + 20%. A boost can be used once; after that its control goes dim. If the same boost is already active, tapping it again does nothing. If the clock hits zero, the status says Time is up! and the round ends.
